# POST_002 — What is a Rust witness?

## Draft Text (LinkedIn Post)

A witness, in the process intelligence sense, is not a person who saw something. It is a typed
artifact that proves a lawful state was reached under a named authority.

CONSTRUCT8 Market Physics does not claim to predict markets. It claims something narrower and more
provable: that two players observing the same market stream reach structurally different state spaces
depending on whether the system is logic-centered or graph-centered.

The logic player sees price and volume. The graph player sees relation breaks, liquidity topology,
event-horizon boundaries, and hidden market bodies — as first-class state.

The Rust witness is the proof that this difference is real, not metaphorical. 35 tests pass. 4
examples run. 8 crates compile. BLAKE3 receipts are verified. The state space difference is
typed — it cannot be compiled away.

What does this have to do with process intelligence?

Everything. In process intelligence, witnesses are how you prove that admissible evidence was
admitted under a named standard — not just that data was loaded. The OCEL witness proves the event
log was admitted under OCEL 2.0, not just parsed. The conformance witness proves the fitness score
was computed by a named algorithm, not just returned by a function.

A system without witnesses can record that things happened. A system with witnesses can prove what
happened under which authority.
