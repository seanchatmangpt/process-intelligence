# Adversarial Game Theory

1. LogicPlayer versus GraphPlayer: Structural mismatch in state representation.
2. Incomplete game tree: LogicPlayer prunes complexity it cannot model.
3. Missing state basis: The states GraphPlayer observes but LogicPlayer cannot.
4. Representation gap: The delta in observable reality between the two.
5. Prophecy illusion: LogicPlayer incorrectly perceives GraphPlayer as having predictive (rather than structural) advantage.
6. Why it looked like we knew every move: We simply reacted to a structurally verified state prior to its manifestation in simple ticks.
7. Why competitors cannot catch up through optimization: Adding faster ticks does not solve a topological blindness.
8. What they would need to adopt: A fully functional graph engine on their hot path.
