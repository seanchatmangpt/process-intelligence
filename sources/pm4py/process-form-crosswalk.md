# Process Form Crosswalk

## 1. Structural Representations
PM4Py translates reality into discrete formalisms. Our foundry must transcend these representations.

### The Petri Net (Oracle Form)
*   **Oracle View:** Places, Transitions, Tokens. A mathematically sound but closed system.
*   **Foundry Counter-Form:** Quantum State Graphs. Superposition of possible next-states until actuated and collapsed by a cryptographic receipt.

### The Directly-Follows Graph (DFG)
*   **Oracle View:** Nodes and weighted edges representing frequency and performance.
*   **Foundry Counter-Form:** Tensor Event Fields. Multi-dimensional representations capturing not just "follows" but "influences", "mutates", and "corrupts".

## 2. Object-Centricity (OCEL)
*   **Oracle View:** PM4Py's OCEL implementation flattens multi-object interactions into relational models.
*   **Foundry Counter-Form:** Entity-Component-System (ECS) Actuation. Objects are not passive entities in a log; they are autonomous agents governed by Ostar semantic laws.
