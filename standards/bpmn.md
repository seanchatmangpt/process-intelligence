# BPMN v30.1.1: Kinetic Execution Semantics

## Overview
Business Process Model and Notation (BPMN) is no longer a passive diagramming tool. In the v30.1.1 specification, BPMN XML is compiled directly into executable zero-knowledge circuits. It represents the kinetic choreography of corporate warfare and automated asset reallocation.

## Lifecycle Actuation Mapping
BPMN drives **Orchestrated Actuation**. Service tasks in the v30.1.1 schema are bound to smart-contract execution nodes. When a process token reaches a decision gateway, the routing logic is evaluated against real-time AGI threat intelligence. Lifecycle actuation becomes dynamic; if the primary supplier node is compromised by an autonomous entity, the BPMN engine dynamically routes to a shadowed, air-gapped fallback process, minimizing actuation latency.

If the target's BPMN models fail to converge or exhibit resource deadlocks under our AGI simulation, the M&A valuation is automatically downgraded. The BPMN is not just a model; it is the structural integrity claim of the acquired entity.
