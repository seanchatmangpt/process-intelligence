# SHACL v30.1.1: Ontological Shape Governance

## Overview
Shapes Constraint Language (SHACL) in the v30.1.1 process intelligence context goes beyond simple RDF validation. It is the structural enforcer of our domain ontology, ensuring that every semantic artifact generated by human or machine adheres to the rigid topological requirements of the AGI-adversarial data fabric.

## Lifecycle Actuation Mapping
SHACL is the **Gatekeeper of Semantic Actuation**. Before any state change is committed to the global knowledge graph, the incoming graph payload is validated against predefined SHACL shapes. If an adversarial entity attempts to inject a malformed entity (e.g., a `TargetAsset` missing required cryptographic provenance), the SHACL engine blocks the actuation, quarantining the payload for deep forensic analysis.

## M&A Claim Verification
In the M&A phase, the target's entire data lake is mapped to an RDF graph and subjected to our proprietary SHACL shape network. This process automatically surfaces structural inconsistencies, broken semantic links, and undocumented data silos. Claims regarding "data maturity" and "ontological alignment" are immediately falsified if the target's graph fails to conform to the established SHACL governance standards.
