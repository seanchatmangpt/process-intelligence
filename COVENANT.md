# Process Intelligence Research Covenant

This covenant defines what this research program studies, what claims it may make, how verdicts
are issued, and what downstream actions it authorizes.

## What Claims May NOT Be Made Without Evidence

The following claim types are FORBIDDEN without supporting evidence:

1. **"wasm4pm supports X"** — requires reading actual source code and confirming the capability exists
2. **"wasm4pm-compat bridges to wasm4pm"** — requires confirming actual import/dependency relationship
3. **"PM4Py cannot do X"** — requires attempting X in PM4Py and confirming absence
4. **"Paper Y grounds claim Z"** — requires reading Paper Y and confirming the grounding
5. **"Gap X exists"** — requires evidence of both the expected capability and its absence
6. **"ALIVE"** — requires all ALIVE gate criteria to be met with evidence
7. **"Fitness >= threshold"** — requires actual pm4py conformance checking output
8. **"Board-admissible"** — requires empirical grounding, academic precedent, and cryptographic receipt

Claims based on assumption, inference, or structural expectation alone are PARTIAL findings, not ALIVE verdicts.

## ALIVE / PARTIAL Verdict Rules

### ALIVE Verdict

An ALIVE verdict certifies that:
1. All gate criteria are met with documentary evidence
2. All claims cite sources (papers, experiments, or prior checkpoints)
3. All receipts are present and chained
4. No gap remains open without a documented mitigation path
5. Downstream authorization is explicitly granted

An ALIVE verdict is signed with the commit hash of the checkpoint file.

### PARTIAL Verdict

A PARTIAL verdict certifies that:
1. Some gate criteria are met and documented
2. The remaining criteria constitute a bill of materials for the next transition
3. No false completion claim has been made
4. The PARTIAL scope is precisely bounded

PARTIAL is not failure — PARTIAL is the bill of materials for the next transition.

### FAILED Verdict

A FAILED verdict certifies that:
1. Evidence contradicts a previously claimed capability
2. A gap is critical and blocking all downstream work
3. An ALIVE verdict was issued without sufficient evidence

A FAILED verdict supersedes any prior ALIVE verdict for the same scope.

## Downstream Authorization Law

**No downstream action may proceed without research program authorization.**

| Downstream Action | Authorization Required |
|---|---|
| wasm4pm algorithm refactor | Research finding confirming gap and remediation path |
| wasm4pm-compat type law change | Research finding confirming the type law basis |
| ggen projection rule change | Research finding grounding the projection in process evidence |
| M&A claim manufacture | Research finding grounding claim in pm4py-verified evidence and paper law |
| New lifecycle state definition | Research finding confirming lifecycle basis in Blue River Dam doctrine |
| New public standards compliance claim | Research finding confirming standard coverage |

**The research program speaks first. Downstream implementations follow.**

This law has no exceptions. A downstream refactor that proceeds without research program authorization
is a defect, not a feature.

---

## Research Program Integrity Rules

1. Never commit unsupported claims to doctrine/ files
2. Every gap document requires: expected capability, observed capability, evidence of absence, severity
3. Every ALIVE verdict requires: all gate criteria met, no open critical gaps, explicit authorization statement
4. Every paper citation requires: paper title, authors, year, and the specific claim it supports
5. Comparison claims (PM4Py vs wasm4pm) require evidence from both systems
6. Conformance scores require actual pm4py execution output, not estimated values
7. Receipt chains must be unbroken — a gap in receipts is a gap in the chain of truth

## The Central Covenant

> Whoever controls admissible process truth controls downstream data, audit, governance,
> automation, intelligence.

This research program exists to ensure that admissible process truth is earned through evidence,
not assumed through declaration. The research program is the upstream dam. Everything downstream
flows from what it admits.
