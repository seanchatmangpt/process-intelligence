\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

In 2016, a language-model experiment demonstrated that fluent text generation is not consequential action.
A system that predicts the next token cannot coordinate the next step.
That lesson became the founding constraint of this research program.

This dissertation advances the thesis that consequential intelligence requires coordination, not prediction alone.
The central claim is formalized as the Chatman Equation: $A = \mu(O^*)$, where agency $A$ is the application of a manufacturing operator $\mu$ to a receipted object graph $O^*$.
Without receipts, there is no verifiable history.
Without verifiable history, there is no lawful basis for action.
Prediction without receipts is not intelligence; it is plausible narrative.

The research program operationalizes this thesis across five interlocking contributions.
First, a process-evidence doctrine grounded in the Van der Aalst Constitution: every declared process must be derivable from an event log, subject to conformance replay, and falsifiable by hostile injection.
Second, a receipt chain architecture in which every manufacturing stage emits a cryptographic receipt, enabling full auditability of object lifecycles from seed through release.
Third, the \texttt{ggen} command grammar and Open Ontologies layer, which provide a declarative surface for manufacturing operators, grounding natural-language commands in receipted, replayable artifact production.
Fourth, \texttt{wasm4pm}, a WebAssembly execution substrate for portable process mining, enabling conformance checking at the edge without institutional infrastructure dependency.
Fifth, a Post-Cyberpunk Process Capital Platform (PCP) and capital-flow architecture that treats process evidence as an investable asset class, establishing an industry-complete loop from raw event log to board-admissible M\&A claim.

The collective contribution is not a theoretical framework.
It is a manufactured, receipted proof: that consequential intelligence, at production scale, requires coordination infrastructure grounded in process evidence --- and that such infrastructure can be built, verified, and released without institutional sponsorship.
