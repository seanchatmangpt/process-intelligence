\chapter*{Acknowledgements}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Acknowledgements}

This research was conducted independently, without institutional affiliation, external funding, or advisory committee.
Every finding was earned by following the evidence.

The orientation throughout has been customer-first.
The measure of success is not citation count, grant volume, or investor return.
It is whether the work makes the next consequential decision more legible, more auditable, and more defensible for the people who bear the cost of getting it wrong.

That standard is harder to meet than any reviewer's rubric.
It is the only one that matters.
