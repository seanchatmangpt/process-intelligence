\section{Implementation Surface}
\label{sec:sources-papers-impl}

\subsection{Source Files}
\label{sec:sources-papers-impl-files}

The \texttt{sources/papers} directory contains 15 files as of the ALIVE gate
assessment (2026-05-31). These files do not contain Rust source code, test
fixtures, or TOML configurations --- those artifacts live in
\texttt{wasm4pm-compat}. The paper corpus files are research documents that
\emph{specify} what must exist in implementation, not implementations
themselves. The distinction is intentional: research authority is separated
from implementation authority.

The primary files and their roles are:

\begin{description}
  \item[\texttt{paper-canon.md} (v30.1.2)] The master conformance registry.
    Provides per-paper conformance claims for 9 core papers with 150+ formal
    objects, 75+ algorithms, 120+ failure conditions, and 25+ board claim
    archetypes. Status: COMPLETE.

  \item[\texttt{PAPER\_CANON\_SCOPE.md}] Scope boundary document defining
    which papers are in-scope for the 81-paper classification and why.

  \item[\texttt{PAPER\_TO\_TYPE\_LAW.md}] The primary obligation surface for
    \texttt{wasm4pm-compat}. Enumerates 18 fully covered papers, 39 at
    graduation boundary, and 10 partial papers with 22 named missing types.

  \item[\texttt{PAPER\_TO\_EXECUTION\_LAW.md}] The primary obligation surface
    for \texttt{wasm4pm}. Enumerates 29 algorithms with input/output type
    shapes; all carry \texttt{NO} wasm4pm coverage, documenting the graduation
    obligation.

  \item[\texttt{PAPER\_TO\_BOARD\_CLAIM.md}] Maps verified type-law coverage
    to board-admissible claims. Five Tier-1 structural completeness claims are
    documented with named evidence paths; five board claims are explicitly
    marked NOT board-admissible due to missing types.

  \item[\texttt{WORKFLOW\_LEDGER.md} / \texttt{workflow-ledger.md}] The
    ledger tracking which papers have been analysed, which obligations have
    been issued, and which receipt events have been emitted.

  \item[\texttt{adversarial-canon.md}] Byzantine-fault-tolerant requirements:
    Sybil attack resistance via BLAKE3 cryptographic receipts, non-deterministic
    reality receipt handling, typestate progression enforcement.

  \item[\texttt{ALPHA\_MINER\_ANALYSIS.md}] Deep analysis of Alpha Miner
    (van der Aalst, Weijters, Maruster 2004) covering PM4Py coverage,
    wasm4pm-compat coverage, and P0 execution gaps. Identifies the critical
    open question: whether \texttt{wasm4pm-algos/src/alpha.rs} incorrectly
    types its output as \texttt{WfNetConst<Sound>}.

  \item[\texttt{INDUCTIVE\_MINER\_ANALYSIS.md}] Confirms that Inductive Miner
    is MISSING from wasm4pm. All board claims about ``sound algorithm
    discovery'' are currently unattested.

  \item[\texttt{OCEL\_2\_0\_ANALYSIS.md}] Documents the 7-object OCEL 2.0
    type surface and confirms full coverage in \texttt{src/ocel.rs}.

  \item[\texttt{DECLARE\_ANALYSIS.md}] Documents the 12 LTLf DECLARE
    templates and their encoding as \texttt{ConstParamTy} variants.

  \item[\texttt{POWL\_ANALYSIS.md}] Documents the POWL 2.0 ChoiceGraph shape
    as absent from \texttt{wasm4pm-compat src/powl.rs} and \texttt{mineDG}
    as absent from wasm4pm. Identifies this as a strategic first-mover
    opportunity since PM4Py mainline also lacks it.

  \item[\texttt{YAWL\_WORKFLOW\_ANALYSIS.md}] Documents YAWL soundness
    (van der Aalst \& ter Hofstede 2004) and the \texttt{WfNetConst<SOUNDNESS>}
    encoding via WOFLAN reduction.

  \item[\texttt{ALIGNMENT\_CONFORMANCE\_ANALYSIS.md}] Documents the
    alignment-based conformance obligation (Adriansyah et al.\ 2011) and
    confirms that \texttt{AlignmentResult}, \texttt{MoveCost}, and
    \texttt{OptimalAlignment} shapes are currently MISSING from
    \texttt{wasm4pm-compat}.

  \item[\texttt{core\_lifecycle\_algorithms.md}] Formalizes four algorithms
    (Paper-to-Law, PM4Py Oracle, Admission Gate, Receipt-Bearing Execution)
    with LaTeX-formatted mathematical foundations.

  \item[\texttt{runtime-verification-wasm.md}] Working paper formalizing Petri
    net soundness, optimal alignment cost minimization ($A^*$-based), and LTLf
    constraint evaluation for WASM execution contexts.

  \item[\texttt{declare-satisfaction-lattice.md}] Defines the 5-valued dynamic
    valuation lattice $\mathcal{V}$ with verified join-semilattice structure.
\end{description}

\subsection{Commands}
\label{sec:sources-papers-impl-commands}

The paper corpus does not expose CLI commands directly. The obligation artifacts
it manufactures are consumed by two downstream CLI surfaces. In
\texttt{wasm4pm-compat}, the command \texttt{cargo test --test ui} runs
trybuild compile-fail and compile-pass fixtures that verify the type-law
obligations derived from the paper corpus. In \texttt{wasm4pm}, the execution
obligation table drives the algorithm implementation backlog.

Within the research foundry, the paper corpus is maintained through the
conventional commit type \texttt{research-paper}, e.g.:
\begin{verbatim}
research-paper: classify Van der Aalst OCEL 2.0 spec → type-law surface
research-paper: document Alpha Miner P0 gap in wasm4pm-algos output type
\end{verbatim}

\subsection{Data and Ontology Surfaces}
\label{sec:sources-papers-impl-data}

The paper corpus does not contain TTL, TOML, or RDF files. Its data surface
is structured Markdown with embedded mathematical notation and explicit
cross-references to Rust source paths. The formal structure is:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Per-paper records}: title, authors, year, formal objects,
    algorithms, failure conditions, compat type-law obligations, wasm4pm
    execution obligations, board claim relevance.
  \item \textbf{Coverage ledger}: 81 papers $\times$ 5 coverage statuses with
    stated reasons for each classification.
  \item \textbf{Obligation backlog}: 22 named missing types, 14 named missing
    output shapes, cross-referenced to specific papers and specific
    \texttt{wasm4pm-compat} source file paths.
  \item \textbf{Graduation reason taxonomy}: Five \texttt{GraduationReason}
    variants (\texttt{NeedsDiscovery}, \texttt{NeedsConformanceExecution},
    \texttt{NeedsObjectCentricQueryExecution}, \texttt{NeedsReceipts},
    \texttt{RebuildingProcessMiningLocally}) mapped to algorithm families and
    specific papers.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Templates and Rendered Artifacts}
\label{sec:sources-papers-impl-templates}

The paper corpus manufactures two classes of rendered artifacts. The first
class is obligation specifications consumed by implementors: the
\texttt{PAPER\_TO\_TYPE\_LAW.md}, \texttt{PAPER\_TO\_EXECUTION\_LAW.md}, and
\texttt{PAPER\_TO\_BOARD\_CLAIM.md} documents function as implementation
contracts. The second class is formal mathematical working papers
(\texttt{runtime-verification-wasm.md}, \texttt{declare-satisfaction-lattice.md},
\texttt{core\_lifecycle\_algorithms.md}) that provide the theoretical grounding
for implementation decisions in \texttt{wasm4pm-compat} and \texttt{wasm4pm}.

No TTL ontology files, no TOML configuration files, and no test fixture files
exist within the papers directory itself. The test fixtures (compile-pass and
compile-fail) that verify the type-law obligations are located in
\texttt{wasm4pm-compat/tests/ui/}, with paths cross-referenced from the paper
corpus documents.
