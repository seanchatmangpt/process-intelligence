\label{sec:sources-wasm4pm-compat-evidence-tests}

and compile-fail fixtures. The ALIVE gate as declared in

compile-fail fixtures (804 total). As of 2026-06-01, the dominant error code across
compile-fail fixtures is \texttt{E0308} (type mismatch, 262 fixtures), followed by

\begin{itemize}

    verifies idempotency ($a \sqcup a = a$) and absorption ($a \sqsubseteq b \Rightarrow
    a \sqcup b = b$); \texttt{test\_witness\_partial\_order()} verifies the partial order
    relation \texttt{witness\_leq}.

    artifact is retrievable and that \texttt{all().len() == 1}.
  \item \texttt{compat/manufacturing/audit.rs}: \texttt{\#[test]} blocks verifying audit
    logic (specific names documented in source; content confirmed present).
  \item \texttt{compat/manufacturing/rendering\_engine.rs}: \texttt{\#[test]} blocks
    verifying Tera rendering paths.
  \item \texttt{compat/manufacturing/toml\_generator.rs}: \texttt{\#[test]} blocks

\end{itemize}

\subsection{ALIVE/PARTIAL/BLOCKED Status}
\label{sec:sources-wasm4pm-compat-evidence-status}

\textbf{Project status: PARTIAL.}

Despite the research-verdict declaring GRADUATION-READY, the status at the dissertation
layer is PARTIAL because two structural defects documented in \texttt{STRUCTURAL\_GAPS.md}
remain open and uncovered by ALIVE-gate fixtures:

\textbf{Defect~1 (High severity): WfNet split-brain.} Two incompatible representations
coexist for WF-net soundness: \texttt{WfNet<S>} (typestate tokens, forgeable) and
\texttt{WfNetConst<const SOUNDNESS: SoundnessState>} (const-generic, non-forgeable). The
\texttt{WfNet<S>.attest\_witnessed()} method is callable from \texttt{WfNet<SoundnessClaimed>}
to \texttt{WfNet<SoundnessWitnessed>} without a proof token. No compile-fail fixture
covers this forgeable path. The ALIVE gate does not witness the most critical soundness
non-forgeability invariant.

\textbf{Defect~2 (Medium severity): Zero multi-step pipeline witness-consistency fixtures.}
The type system structurally prevents a \texttt{Refusal<R, Ocel20>} from being confused
with a \texttt{Refusal<R, Xes1849>} in a chained pipeline, but no compile-fail fixture
receipts this guarantee end-to-end in a multi-stage admission sequence. Under the Van der
Aalst constitution, an unreceipted guarantee is a structural claim, not a witnessed fact.

Until these two defects are closed with receipted compile-fail fixtures, the ALIVE verdict
cannot be issued. The PARTIAL status is the accurate characterization of the project's
current standing within the dissertation evidence chain.
