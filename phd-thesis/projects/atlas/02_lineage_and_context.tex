\subsection{2016 Language-Model Lesson}
\label{sec:atlas-lineage-2016}

The thesis lineage begins with a 2016 language-model experiment whose conclusion
was that local text imitation does not conserve consequence. A language model
that can reproduce the surface form of a process description --- its vocabulary,
its syntactic structure, its domain terminology --- cannot thereby reproduce the
causal and evidential structure of the process itself. Text imitation is
consequence-free: it can describe a transition without actuating one, name a
receipt without emitting one, and reference a lifecycle stage without
receipting it.

This lesson is the negative foundation of the atlas. Every doctrine-surface file
in the atlas encodes a module whose core claim is that description alone is
insufficient. The Aalst Broadcaster does not describe Git history; it transforms
it into an OCEL stream with real event objects and timestamps. The GGEN Prompt
Manufactory does not paraphrase research papers; it manufactures research
warrants through a typed transformation chain (TTL $\to$ RQ $\to$ TERA $\to$
Prompt) that is itself receipted. The Blue River Dam does not assert that a
process is ALIVE; it gates every transition through $\kappa \circ \rho \circ
\alpha \circ \mu$ and refuses to produce an ungated outcome. In each case, the
module exists because the 2016 lesson made clear that the description-based
alternative is epistemically empty.

\subsection{Enterprise Process Gap}
\label{sec:atlas-lineage-gap}

The enterprise process gap that followed from the 2016 lesson was this: large
organizations possess extensive process documentation --- BPMN models, SOP
libraries, audit logs, compliance reports --- but lack the machinery to
manufacture board-admissible process claims from that documentation. The
documentation describes; it does not evidence. When a buyer in an M\&A due
diligence engagement asks whether a declared process actually ran as declared,
the seller can produce documents but not receipts.

The atlas encodes this gap at the module level. The Supabase Law Substrate
establishes that ``React state is not authorization'' because enterprise
applications routinely mistake client-side state for server-side enforcement.
The WASM Boundary Law separates the Specta type surface from the
tsify/wasm-bindgen binary surface because enterprise WebAssembly deployments
frequently conflate type-level promises with binary-level guarantees. The ZoeApp
Ministry Ontology encodes Connect, Give, Watch, Serve, and Pray as process-laws
--- not as UI labels --- because ministry operations conducted without process
law have no basis for lifecycle accountability. Each atlas module names a
specific form of the enterprise process gap and the doctrine required to close
it.

\subsection{Relationship to the Chatman Equation}
\label{sec:atlas-lineage-chatman}

The Chatman Equation, as formalized in
\texttt{doctrine/PROCESS\_INTELLIGENCE\_SPR\_THESIS.md}, is:

\[
  A = \mu(O^*, T, L)
\]

where $\mu$ is the manufacture operator, $O^*$ is the knowledge corpus
(public standards, type laws, research receipts), $T$ is the transition set, and
$L$ is the lifecycle law. The atlas maps each of the seven doctrine modules to
this equation as follows:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Aalst Broadcaster} supplies the event log stream that populates
        $O^*$ with empirical evidence from Git history.
  \item \textbf{GGEN Prompt Manufactory} implements $\mu$ at the research
        warrant level: TTL $\to$ RQ $\to$ TERA $\to$ Prompt is a typed
        instantiation of the manufacture operator applied to ontology.
  \item \textbf{Blue River Dam} enforces the gate operator $\kappa$ such that
        $\kappa(\rho(\alpha(\mu(O^*)))) \in \{\textsc{ALIVE}, \textsc{PARTIAL},
        \textsc{REFUSED}\}$ --- no ungated outcome is admissible.
  \item \textbf{Supabase Law Substrate} enforces $\kappa$ at the data layer:
        every SQL command is gated by RLS policy, not by client-side projection.
  \item \textbf{WASM Boundary Law} enforces $T$ at the type membrane: Specta
        governs the type surface (TypeScript control), tsify/wasm-bindgen governs
        the binary surface (WASM ABI), and conflation of the two is a refused
        transition.
  \item \textbf{ZoeApp Ministry Ontology} applies $L$ to a ministry domain:
        the lifecycle stages healthy, degraded, and critical constitute a formal
        recovery path governed by process law.
  \item \textbf{AGI Adversarial Defenses} bounds the system against
        complexity-collapse: Schr\"odinger's Interface Fallback and Turing Trap
        Circuit Breakers are refusal mechanisms that enforce $\kappa$ against
        adversarial inputs.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Relationship to Receipts and Replay}
\label{sec:atlas-lineage-receipts}

The receipt chain for the foundry is defined in
\texttt{receipts/RECEIPT\_REGISTRY.md} as seven canonical receipts:
PAPER\_CANON\_RECEIPT, PM4PY\_ORACLE\_RECEIPT, WASM4PM\_GAP\_RECEIPT,
LIFECYCLE\_RECEIPT, MA\_RECEIPT, STANDARDS\_RECEIPT, and
ADVERSARIAL\_RECEIPT. Each receipt certifies that a named criterion was met with
a specific evidence path, a named witness (law, standard, or paper), and an
enumerated set of open gaps.

The atlas doctrine surfaces relate to this receipt chain at the naming level:
the atlas names the modules whose execution produces or relies on these receipts.
The LIFECYCLE\_RECEIPT, for example, certifies 42 lifecycle phase definitions
covering the full Lifecycle Calculus
(Design $\to$ Simulation $\to$ Construction $\to$ Activation $\to$ Operation
$\to$ Monitoring $\to$ Repair $\to$ Optimization $\to$ BoardProjection $\to$
Integration $\to$ Decommission $\to$ Archive). The ZoeApp Ministry Ontology and
the Blue River Dam doctrine modules are the primary consumers of this receipt in
the atlas. Neither module can claim a lawful lifecycle transition without the
LIFECYCLE\_RECEIPT establishing that the phase definitions were manufactured and
sealed.

The atlas itself does not produce a receipt. This is by design: it is a
navigational surface, not a manufacturing operator. Its PARTIAL status reflects
the absence of an internal receipt, not a failure of the modules it maps.
