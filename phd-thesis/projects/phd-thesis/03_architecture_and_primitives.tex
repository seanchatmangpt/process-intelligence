\subsection{Core Objects}
\label{sec:phd-thesis-arch-objects}

carrying an absolute path, a human-readable description, detected languages, detected

\subsection{Admissible Transitions}
\label{sec:phd-thesis-arch-transitions}

The workspace defines admissible state transitions through its ALIVE criteria. The current
state is \texttt{SETUP\_COMPLETE}. The admissible transitions from this state are:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{SETUP\_COMPLETE $\to$ CHAPTER\_MANUFACTURING}: Triggered when TeX source
    files are written to \texttt{chapters/} and \texttt{frontmatter/}. The trigger must
    produce a receipt recording the manufacturing event, the source commit, and the ggen
    configuration used.
  \item \textbf{CHAPTER\_MANUFACTURING $\to$ PDF\_COMPILED}: Triggered when
    \texttt{pdflatex} compiles the chapter sources without error. The compiled PDF hash
    must be recorded in the receipt chain.
  \item \textbf{PDF\_COMPILED $\to$ ALIVE}: Triggered when all ALIVE criteria are satisfied:
    TeX files present, source ledger populated, claim ledger respected, PDF compiled, PDF
    hash recorded.
\end{enumerate}

These transitions mirror the broader lifecycle model defined in
\texttt{lifecycle/MATURITY\_MODEL.md} and the MAPE-K loop closure documented in
\texttt{blue\_river\_dam/src/lib.rs}. The workspace is a process object; its lifecycle is
governed by the same doctrine as every other artifact in the foundry.

\subsection{Boundaries}
\label{sec:phd-thesis-arch-boundaries}

The workspace enforces two categories of boundary. The first is the \textbf{claim boundary}:
no claim may appear in any manufactured artifact without a backing source file, commit, or
explicit marking as AUTHOR THESIS, INTERPRETATION, or FUTURE WORK. This boundary is enforced
by the DO\_NOT\_CLAIM\_LEDGER and is the workspace-level instantiation of the broader
doctrine in \texttt{doctrine/CONFORMANCE\_AS\_LAW.md} and
\texttt{doctrine/RECEIPT\_DOCTRINE.md}.

The second is the \textbf{release boundary}: no downstream use of artifacts from this workspace
is authorized until the research program issues authorization. As stated in
\texttt{phd-thesis/README.md}: ``No downstream use of artifacts from this workspace is
authorized until the research program issues authorization.'' The authorization prerequisite
has been met by PI\_RESEARCH\_PROGRAM\_ALIVE\_001 (sealed 2026-06-01, 12/12 gates PASS),
but the workspace itself is not yet ALIVE --- it has not yet produced its primary artifact
(the compiled dissertation PDF with recorded hash). Release is therefore blocked until the
PDF compilation phase completes and the ALIVE criteria are satisfied.

\subsection{Failure Modes Refused}
\label{sec:phd-thesis-arch-refusals}

The workspace explicitly refuses three categories of failure mode. First, \textbf{forced ALIVE
verdicts}: an artifact cannot be declared ALIVE by assertion. ALIVE requires all five criteria
to be met. This refusal is architecturally enforced by the DO\_NOT\_CLAIM\_LEDGER's ALIVE
definition, which specifies exactly what evidence must exist. The broader research program
observed this discipline in Gate 12 of PI\_RESEARCH\_PROGRAM\_ALIVE\_001: ``No Forced ALIVE
Verdicts --- All ALIVE verdicts justified by passed gates.''

Second, \textbf{claim without receipt}: no M\&A claim, no conformance score, and no
fitness/precision figure may appear in any dissertation artifact unless it is backed by a
receipt in the \texttt{receipts/} directory. The receipt must carry a process model hash,
an event log hash, and a validator signature. The five canonical receipts
(\texttt{rec\_ebitda\_rework\_001.json} through \texttt{rec\_residual\_standard\_005.json})
establish the pattern that all dissertation claims must follow.

Third, \textbf{scope collapse}: the workspace refuses to treat the dissertation as a
description of the research program rather than a manufactured artifact of it. The distinction
matters: a description can be written without receipts; a manufactured artifact cannot be
released without them. The workspace is a manufacturing authority, not a documentation surface,
and its failure modes are therefore the failure modes of manufacturing --- wrong toolchain,
incomplete receipt chain, ALIVE criteria not met --- not the failure modes of writing.
