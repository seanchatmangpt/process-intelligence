\subsection{Known Gaps}
\label{sec:phd-thesis-open-gaps}

The following gaps are documented in the evidence extracted for this workspace. Each is an
honest gap record, not a prediction or a claim about future resolution.

\textbf{Gap 1: TeX sources and compiled PDF do not exist.}
The \texttt{chapters/}, \texttt{frontmatter/}, \texttt{build/}, and \texttt{scripts/}
directories are all empty stubs. No TeX chapter sources have been manufactured. No master
TeX file has been written. No pdflatex compilation has been attempted. The ALIVE criteria
(criteria 4 and 5: PDF compiles without error; PDF hash recorded) are not met. This is the
primary structural gap of the workspace.

\textbf{Gap 2: WORKFLOW\_RECEIPT.yaml phase is SETUP\_COMPLETE with no manufacturing phase.}
The workflow receipt records only the setup phase. No manufacturing phase receipt exists.
When chapter manufacture begins, a new phase receipt must be emitted and recorded in the
receipt chain. The mechanism for this emission --- whether ggen, a custom script, or a
manual receipt entry --- has not been specified.

\textbf{Gap 3: BLAKE3 toolchain availability not recorded.}
The receipt doctrine requires BLAKE3 hashing of the compiled PDF. The WORKFLOW\_RECEIPT.yaml
records pdflatex, Python, git, and ripgrep, but does not record a BLAKE3 tool. This must be
remediated before the PDF compilation phase can produce a valid receipt.

\textbf{Gap 4: No ggen.toml for the thesis chapter pipeline.}
The ggen manufacturing engine (\texttt{ggen/ggen.toml}) exists and is operational, but no
thesis-chapter-specific ggen.toml has been confirmed present in the \texttt{phd-thesis}
workspace. The open question recorded in the evidence is: is ggen the intended chapter
manufacturing engine, and does a ggen.toml for the thesis chapter pipeline exist? At the
time of writing, the answer is no.

\textbf{Gap 5: 2016 LM experiment not documented in the 34 project manifests.}
The thesis lineage in \texttt{ledgers/PROJECT\_INDEX.yaml} begins with the 2016
language-model experiment, but this experiment is not documented in any of the 34 project
manifests or receipts found in the foundry. Its source materials predate the
\texttt{process-intelligence} repository. The dissertation must either locate the experiment
evidence, create a manifest entry for it, or clearly mark all citations of it as AUTHOR
THESIS.

\textbf{Gap 6: DTO flattening remediation in wasm4pm-compat is open.}
As documented in PI\_RESEARCH\_PROGRAM\_ALIVE\_001, the \texttt{to\_json\_string()} and
\texttt{receipt\_json()} methods in \texttt{compat/src/manufacturing/} violate the FFI
boundary law. This is non-blocking to Program ALIVE and non-blocking to the dissertation
workspace, but any dissertation chapter that cites GGEN\_ECOSYSTEM\_INTEL as fully ALIVE
must reflect that GGEN\_ECOSYSTEM\_INTEL\_ALIVE\_001 has 3/4 audits PASS with 1
remediation open.

manufacturing phase will need to produce its own receipt chain --- for each chapter
manufactured, for the PDF compilation, and for the final artifact hash. This is a new receipt
