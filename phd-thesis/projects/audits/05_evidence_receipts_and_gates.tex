\section{Evidence, Receipts, and Gates}
\label{sec:audits-evidence}

\subsection{Tests}
\label{sec:audits-evidence-tests}

The test evidence base for the audits corpus is substantial and forensically
verified.

\textbf{Core Rust test suite: 84 passing tests.} The forensic audit verdict
(\texttt{forensic\_audit\_verdict.md}) confirms 84 total tests across three
crates, all passing with zero failures: 54 tests in \texttt{sources/wasm4pm}
(comprising 23 core unit tests, 10 end-to-end tests, and 21 integration tests),
7 tests in \texttt{blue\_river\_dam}, and 23 tests in
\texttt{sources/wasm4pm-compat/compat}. The forensic verdict is \textsc{clean}:
no stubs, no mocks, no hardcoded outputs, no pre-populated result artifacts.

\textbf{A* alignment correctness: 3 scenarios verified.} The alignment referee
audit documents three test cases against a standard Register $\to$ Approve $\to$
Ship workflow net. Case~1 (fully conforming trace \texttt{["Register", "Approve",
"Ship"]}): cost~0, resolution time 89.667~\textmu s. Case~2 (model omission,
missing "Register"): cost~1, resolution time 60.958~\textmu s. Case~3 (log
insertion, extra "Audit"): cost~1, resolution time 72.708~\textmu s. All three
resolve well under the 10~ms ceiling; the empirical average is approximately
74~\textmu s, satisfying a 200$\times$ performance margin.

\textbf{EWMA drift detection: 19 empirical cases.} The drift sentry audit
documents Cases~1 through~19 of an empirical EWMA drift detection simulation.
The EWMA parameters (\(\lambda = 0.20\), \(\mu_0 = 1.00\), \(\sigma_0 = 0.08\),
\(L = 3\), \(LCL = 0.92\)) are mathematically derived in the audit and confirmed
to trigger the drift alert within 4 traces of injected drift events.

\textbf{SHA-256 ledger equivalence: Rust integration test.} The
\texttt{test\_sha256\_ledger\_compliance} Rust integration test, added in Epoch
V30.1.1, verifies that the block serialization format from
\texttt{experiments/visualizer/ledger.js} produces byte-identical SHA-256 output
to the pure-Rust implementation in \texttt{sources/wasm4pm/src/crypto.rs}.

\textbf{Compile-fail fixture corpus: 199 fixtures.} The 199 compile-fail
fixtures in \texttt{sources/wasm4pm-compat/tests/ui/compile\_fail/} constitute
the type-law receipt corpus. Error code distribution: \texttt{E0308} (262
occurrences, type mismatch), \texttt{E0277} (20 occurrences, trait bound not
satisfied), \texttt{E0599} (10 occurrences, method not found), \texttt{E0451}
(2 occurrences, private field access). Zero \texttt{E0425} fixtures. Valid
law-proving coverage: 97.7\% (292 of 299 error occurrences).

\textbf{WF-net soundness: DFS reachability tests.} The Petri net soundness audit
documents that \texttt{PetriNet::analyze\_soundness} implements three invariant
checks via DFS reachability: option to complete (backward traversal from sink
marking), proper completion (sink tokens must equal exactly 1 with all other
places at 0), and no dead transitions (all transitions must appear in
\(\mathcal{T}_{\text{fired}}\)). The \texttt{MAX\_STATES} hard ceiling of 10,000
prevents state-space explosion.
