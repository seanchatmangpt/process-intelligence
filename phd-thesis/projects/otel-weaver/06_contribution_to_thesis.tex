\section{Contribution to the Dissertation}
\label{sec:otel-weaver-contribution}

\subsection{Contribution to Prediction vs.~Coordination}
\label{sec:otel-weaver-contribution-prediction}

The dissertation's central tension between prediction and coordination surfaces acutely at the
ingestion boundary. Prediction-mode systems---those that produce probabilistic outputs from
statistical models over historical data---can operate adequately on raw telemetry. A latency
anomaly predictor does not need court-grade evidence; it needs sufficient signal. Coordination-mode
systems---those that assert, route, settle, or certify process outcomes---require evidence that
can survive independent replay and adversarial audit.

The \texttt{otel-weaver} ingestion layer is the architectural mechanism that enforces this
distinction at the data boundary. By requiring every span to traverse an \texttt{Admission<T,\,W>}
gate before entering the coordination layer, the project prevents prediction-grade feedstock
from contaminating coordination-grade claims. This is not a performance optimization; it is a
correctness requirement. A process conformance verdict computed over admitted feedstock is a
different epistemic object than a verdict computed over raw telemetry, even if the underlying
span data is identical. The typed gate makes this difference structural and auditable.

The doctrine constant \texttt{OBSERVABILITY\_VS\_PROCESS\_INTELLIGENCE\_001} articulates this
distinction for the dissertation record: observability captures what the system emitted;
process intelligence certifies what the process did. The \texttt{otel-weaver} project provides
the formal mechanism---not merely the conceptual vocabulary---for enforcing this distinction
at the ingestion layer of a live distributed system.

\subsection{Contribution to Process Evidence}
\label{sec:otel-weaver-contribution-evidence}

The project's most direct contribution to the dissertation's process-evidence framework is the
formalization of the feedstock-to-evidence promotion path and the typed naming of its failure
modes. Prior to this formalization, the Process Intelligence Research Foundry could assert that
telemetry is not evidence, but had no type-system enforcement of that assertion. The
\texttt{Admission<T,\,W>} / \texttt{Refusal} gate architecture provides that enforcement.

The \texttt{LossReport} / \texttt{OtelToOcelLossPolicy} apparatus contributes a second layer:
the formal acknowledgment that projection from OTel spans to OCEL events is \emph{necessarily
lossy} and that the losses must be declared, not concealed. This is a contribution to the
dissertation's treatment of evidence chain integrity. An event log derived through a declared
\texttt{LossReport} is epistemically superior to one derived silently, even if both logs contain
identical data, because the declared log's analyst knows what was discarded.

The Replay Test criterion---requiring independent third-party replayability without
visualization---operationalizes the dissertation's evidence standard for the ingestion layer.
It establishes that the \texttt{otel-weaver} pipeline's output must be sufficient for replay,
not merely for display. This criterion anchors the project's success condition to a
falsifiable, third-party-verifiable standard rather than to internal consistency alone.

\subsection{Contribution to Manufacturing Architecture}
\label{sec:otel-weaver-contribution-manufacturing}

Within the foundry's manufacturing architecture, \texttt{otel-weaver} occupies the stratum
immediately upstream of the \texttt{wasm4pm} execution engine. Its contribution is to ensure
that the \texttt{wasm4pm} execution engine receives only typed, witnessed, loss-declared payloads.
This enables the manufacturing pipeline to reason about its inputs with full type safety: every
\texttt{Admission<T,\,W>} that reaches \texttt{wasm4pm} is known to have passed the
\texttt{process.pi} registry live-check, to carry an \texttt{ActivityWitness}, and to be
accompanied by a \texttt{LossReport}.

The \texttt{BridgeRx} artifact contributes schema-version stability to the manufacturing pipeline.
By ensuring $R_\Delta = 0$ across schema migrations, it prevents schema evolution from
introducing measurement error into the conformance checking layer. This is a manufacturing
quality gate: it certifies that the pipeline's conformance fitness scores are attributable to
process behavior rather than to schema drift.

The \texttt{ggen} receipt chain manufactured by the two project manifests contributes to the
foundry's BLAKE3 chain integrity. Each receipt anchors a manufacturing run, providing an
immutable record of what was ingested, when, and under which schema version. This chain is
the cryptographic backbone of the project's contribution to the foundry's audit surface.

\subsection{Contribution to Capital-Flow Infrastructure}
\label{sec:otel-weaver-contribution-capital}

The capital-flow and settlement layer of the dissertation's architecture requires process evidence
that can withstand adversarial audit: a counterparty challenging a settlement claim must be unable
to argue that the underlying process evidence was derived from observability signal rather than
from a conforming process execution. The \texttt{otel-weaver} ingestion layer provides the
first line of defense against this challenge.

By enforcing that every piece of process evidence entering the downstream pipeline has passed
through a typed gate backed by a BLAKE3-chained receipt, the project ensures that capital-flow
claims can trace their evidentiary provenance to the ingestion boundary. A claim that cites
a process receipt can, in principle, be traced back through the receipt chain to the
\texttt{Admission<T,\,W>} gate that admitted the underlying feedstock, to the
\texttt{weaver registry live-check} that validated its schema, and to the raw OTel span
that entered the pipeline.

This provenance chain is a prerequisite for the dissertation's capital-flow infrastructure
to operate without the enterprise process gap. Without \texttt{otel-weaver}'s ingestion
boundary, the capital-flow layer would be built on observability signal dressed as process
evidence---the exact failure mode that motivated the 2016 language-model lineage observation
and the foundry's foundational design.
