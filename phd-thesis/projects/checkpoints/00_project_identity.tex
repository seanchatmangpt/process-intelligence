\section{Project Identity}
\label{sec:checkpoints-identity}

\subsection{Purpose}
\label{sec:checkpoints-identity-purpose}

The \texttt{checkpoints/} directory is the phase milestone verdict ledger for the Process Intelligence Research
Foundry. Its purpose is not to record activity but to certify consequence: every checkpoint file is an
immutable declaration that a named set of audit gates has been evaluated, that evidence was counted against
minimum thresholds, and that the resulting verdict---ALIVE, PARTIAL, or FAILED---stands permanently as issued.

Verdict permanence is a design requirement, not a convention. The ledger operates under the
\emph{no forced ALIVE} rule: a verdict of ALIVE may only be sealed when all enumerated gate criteria
are simultaneously met. A single unmet threshold produces a PARTIAL verdict. PARTIAL is not failure;
the RESEARCH\_CRITERIA.md file encodes the explicit doctrine: \textquotedblleft PARTIAL is not failure
--- PARTIAL is the bill of materials for the next transition.\textquotedblright{} The FAILED category
exists for verdicts where evidence is not merely incomplete but contradicts the claim.

The ledger is also an authorization instrument. Each ALIVE checkpoint carries an explicit list of
downstream workflows that are authorized by its sealing, and an equally explicit list of workflows
that are not yet authorized. No downstream manufacturing may proceed against unadmitted evidence,
unwitnessed conformance scores, or receipts outside the registry.

\subsection{Repository Surface}
\label{sec:checkpoints-identity-surface}

The \texttt{checkpoints/} directory resides at the root of the \texttt{process-intelligence} repository.
Its principal source files are Markdown checkpoint documents, each named with a checkpoint identifier
that encodes verdict type and sequence number. As of 2026-06-01 the directory contains the following
sealed and open documents: \texttt{PROCESS\_INTELLIGENCE\_ALIVE\_001.md}, \texttt{ALIVE\_GATE\_ASSESSMENT.md},
\texttt{PI\_RESEARCH\_PROGRAM\_ALIVE\_001.md}, \texttt{GGEN\_ECOSYSTEM\_INTEL\_ALIVE\_001.md},
\texttt{SUBSTRATE\_COMPLETE\_001.md}, \texttt{RESEARCH\_CRITERIA.md},
\texttt{GGEN\_OTEL\_WEAVER\_PI\_ALIVE\_001.md}, \texttt{GGEN\_OTEL\_WEAVER\_PI\_PARTIAL\_001.md},
\texttt{GGEN\_OTEL\_WEAVER\_PI\_RUNTIME\_001.md}, \texttt{PROCESS\_INTELLIGENCE\_PARTIAL\_001.md},
\texttt{PROCESS\_INTELLIGENCE\_ADVERSARIAL\_V30.1.1\_OMEGA.md}, \texttt{PETRI\_NET\_SNAPSHOT.md}, and
\texttt{LIVESTREAM\_STATUS\_001.md}.

The companion \texttt{receipts/} directory holds the cryptographic receipt chain that each ALIVE
checkpoint references. Receipts are BLAKE3- or SHA-256-sealed documents certifying that a named
manufacturing operator ran, produced its artifact, and logged the result under a specific witness marker.
Seven canonical research program receipts are indexed in \texttt{receipts/RECEIPT\_REGISTRY.md}.

\subsection{Primary Research Role}
\label{sec:checkpoints-identity-role}

Within the dissertation, the checkpoint ledger occupies the position of \emph{process truth authority}.
It does not describe process mining; it enacts the process mining discipline on the research program
itself. Every ALIVE verdict is a conformance check result: the declared process model is the gate
criteria table; the event log is the artifact inventory; the conformance score is the gate pass ratio.
When the ALIVE\_GATE\_ASSESSMENT document records 12 of 12 criteria met, that is a fitness value of 1.0
against the declared research process model.

This self-application of process intelligence doctrine to the research program's own lifecycle is the
primary contribution of the checkpoint subsystem to the thesis argument. It demonstrates that the
doctrine is operational---that it can be applied to a real knowledge-production process, not merely
described as a theoretical framework.
