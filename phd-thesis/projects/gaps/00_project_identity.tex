\section{Project Identity}

\subsection{Purpose}
\label{sec:gaps-identity-purpose}

The \texttt{gaps/} directory is the structural gap registry for the
\texttt{process-intelligence} research foundry. Its purpose is to maintain a permanent,
immutable record of every named defect identified between the wasm4pm-compat type-law
enforcement layer and the wasm4pm execution engine. Under the immutability doctrine
inherited from \texttt{process-intelligence} governance, gap records are never deleted
or overwritten; they may only be extended by dated resolution addendums. A gap that
carries no resolution addendum is formally open, regardless of any informal claim to the
contrary. This single rule ensures that the registry functions as a tamper-evident audit
surface: board-level claims about type-law enforcement, process-evidence provenance, and
algorithm soundness can be falsified by consulting the gap register and verifying that the
gap blocking the claim has been formally closed.

The registry is not aspirational documentation. Each gap entry contains direct code
citations — Cargo.toml excerpts, Rust type signatures, and error enum variants —
that constitute empirical proof of the defect. GAP\_001, the most critical entry,
reproduces the \texttt{wasm4pm/Cargo.toml} dependency block verbatim to demonstrate that
\texttt{wasm4pm-compat} does not appear in it, thereby proving the bridge is entirely
absent rather than merely incomplete.

No receipt files, checkpoint files, ontology files, or test fixtures reside in the
\texttt{gaps/} directory itself. Evidence is purely documentary and analytical.
Resolution artifacts, when produced, are expected to materialize in the broader
\texttt{receipts/} and \texttt{checkpoints/} directories of the research foundry.

\subsection{Primary Research Role}
\label{sec:gaps-identity-role}

Within the dissertation, the gap registry serves the role of \emph{structural-gap-registry-and-closure-tracking}.
It provides the evidentiary basis for every claim that the current implementation stack
is incomplete. The registry makes falsifiability concrete: a reader who disputes a
``type-law enforcement is absent'' claim can navigate to GAP\_001 and examine the Cargo.toml
citation. Without the gap registry, the dissertation's architectural claims rest on
positive assertion alone. With it, they rest on artifact-grounded defect records that
follow the Van der Aalst Constitution's requirement that every conformance verdict be
derivable from observable evidence rather than from code inspection or verbal testimony.

The registry also establishes the ordering authority for downstream remediation work.
No wasm4pm refactor is authorized until the research program approves the remediation
path documented in the relevant gap record.
