\section{Evidence, Receipts, and Gates}
\label{sec:gaps-evidence}

\subsection{Tests}
\label{sec:gaps-evidence-tests}

No test files reside in the \texttt{gaps/} directory. The gap registry is a documentary
surface, not an executable surface. The tests that would validate gap closure are
located in the remediating projects: compile-fail fixtures in wasm4pm-compat that verify
type-law enforcement (currently compromised by E0425 failures as documented in GAP\_008),
and integration tests in wasm4pm that would confirm \texttt{Evidence<EventLog, Admitted, W>}
is accepted at algorithm boundaries once GAP\_001 is closed.

GAP\_008 is itself a gap about the quality of test receipts. It documents that some
\texttt{compile\_fail} fixtures in \texttt{tests/ui/compile\_fail/} fail on E0425 (cannot
find type in scope) rather than on structural law violations. The correct error codes for
true type-law receipts are: E0308 (type mismatch — wrong state or witness type); E0599
(method not found for type — law not satisfied); E0277 (trait bound not satisfied —
compile-time predicate rejected); E0080 (const eval failure — \texttt{generic\_const\_exprs}
bound violated). Until GAP\_008 is closed, the test surface of wasm4pm-compat cannot
be cited as evidence of type-law enforcement without reservation.

\subsection{Receipts}
\label{sec:gaps-evidence-receipts}

No receipt files exist in the \texttt{gaps/} directory. Under the immutability doctrine,
gap closure is expected to produce resolution addendum documents in the individual gap
files and corresponding checkpoint receipts in the foundry's \texttt{receipts/} and
\texttt{checkpoints/} directories. At the time of this writing, no such receipts have
been produced for any of the eight gap records.

The absence of receipts is directly connected to the PARTIAL status of this component.
GAP\_001 documents that the receipt chain between wasm4pm-compat admission and wasm4pm
execution is broken: ``receipts from wasm4pm-compat admission cannot be linked to wasm4pm
execution'' (\texttt{GAP\_001\_COMPAT\_WASM\_BRIDGE.md}, Impact Assessment). This means
that even if compat produces admission receipts for individual events, those receipts
cannot be chained forward into wasm4pm execution artifacts. The receipt chain is severed
at the boundary that GAP\_001 identifies.

\subsection{Checkpoints}
\label{sec:gaps-evidence-checkpoints}

No checkpoint files exist in the \texttt{gaps/} directory. Checkpoints in the
\texttt{process-intelligence} foundry represent phase milestone verdicts (ALIVE, PARTIAL,
FAILED) and are permanent records under the immutability doctrine. For the gap registry
component, a checkpoint would require that a defined set of gap closure conditions are
all demonstrably met, with receipts and resolution addendums present to support the
verdict.

No such checkpoint can currently be issued. The dependency graph in
\texttt{GAP\_PRIORITY\_MATRIX.md} establishes that the critical path
(\texttt{GAP\_007 $\rightarrow$ GAP\_001 $\rightarrow$ GAP\_002}) must be traversed before
the board-level claims that depend on these gaps can be made. None of the three critical-path
gaps have resolution addendums. GAP\_002 has a resolution described in its own document
(v30.1.1 Smart-Completion Policy) but the discrepancy between that document's Resolved
status and the \texttt{GAP\_REGISTER.md} Open status has not been reconciled by a
formal addendum, so the register remains authoritative in the OPEN state.

\subsection{ALIVE/PARTIAL/BLOCKED Status}
\label{sec:gaps-evidence-status}

\textbf{Status: PARTIAL.}

The gap registry achieves PARTIAL status for the following reasons:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Present and substantive:} Five gap documents exist with detailed,
        code-cited content. The registry accurately identifies eight structural defects.
        The dependency graph correctly sequences the closure order. The blocking-claims
        table truthfully maps claims to their prerequisite gaps. The priority matrix
        provides an actionable P1/P2/P3 sequence. This documentary surface is complete
        and well-grounded.

  \item \textbf{Absent:} No gap has a formal resolution addendum with an accompanying
        receipt. No checkpoint receipt has been issued for any gap closure. The discrepancy
        between GAP\_002's individual document (Resolved) and the master register (Open)
        has not been formally reconciled. GAP\_007's closure workflow entry (described as
        Resolved in \texttt{GAP\_CLOSURE\_WORKFLOW.md}) contradicts the master register's
        Open status with no reconciling addendum.

  \item \textbf{Cannot claim ALIVE:} ALIVE status requires receipts, tests, and a
        checkpoint, none of which exist for this component. The gap registry is accurate
        and trustworthy as a documentary surface, but it has not yet produced any
        verifiable closure artifacts.
\end{itemize}

The PARTIAL verdict is consistent with the research program's general finding that the
wasm4pm-compat / wasm4pm integration is architecturally described but not yet
implemented. The gap registry is the correct and authoritative documentation of that
incompleteness.
