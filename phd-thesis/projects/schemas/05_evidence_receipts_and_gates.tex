\section{Evidence, Receipts, and Gates}
\label{sec:schemas-evidence}

\subsection{Tests}
\label{sec:schemas-evidence-tests}

No automated test harness exists for the schemas project at time of assessment. The research
program's \texttt{TEST\_INFRA.md} specification defines an E2E test philosophy grounded in three
principles---opaque-box enforcement, mathematical invariant assertions, and forensic
admissibility---but does not instantiate a test case that validates receipt files against the
\texttt{SlideVerificationLedger} schema.

Adversarial test scripts exist in the \texttt{adversarial/} directory:

\begin{description}
  \item[\texttt{adversarial/validate\_standards.py}] Validates that standards files carry required
    fields. Does not validate receipt JSON against the ledger schema.
  \item[\texttt{adversarial/simulate\_laundering\_spoofing.py}] Simulates raw log laundering and
    hash spoofing attacks. Tests the adversarial robustness of the receipt chain concept but
    does not execute schema validation.
  \item[\texttt{experiments/validate\_ocpq\_refinement.py}] Validates OCPQ subset inclusion.
    Not schema-related.
  \item[\texttt{experiments/validate\_log\_fitness.py}] Validates log fitness metrics.
    Not schema-related.
\end{description}

The \texttt{TEST\_INFRA.md} integrity mandate explicitly states: ``No test case shall utilize mock
validators, dummy signatures, hardcoded hashes, or stubbed state machines.'' By this standard,
the five production receipts in \texttt{receipts/}---which carry hardcoded hashes and sample
signatures manufactured for schema demonstration---do not meet the integrity mandate for live
test evidence. They satisfy structural conformance but not execution conformance.

\subsection{Receipts}
\label{sec:schemas-evidence-receipts}

Five receipts in \texttt{receipts/} serve as the primary evidence base for the schemas project.
Each receipt demonstrates that the \texttt{SlideVerificationLedger} schema is practicable for
real M\&A claims:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{rec\_ebitda\_rework\_001.json} --- Slide 1 (EBITDA). \texttt{slide\_id}:
    \texttt{8a3e811c-2290-482a-a5f1-3215903b41fa}. Fitness: 0.982. Precision: 0.945.
    EBITDA impact: \$1,250,000. Behavioral constraint: Invoice Approval must follow Goods Receipt
    (compliance rate: 1.0).
  \item \textbf{rec\_wc\_ar\_002.json} --- Slide 2 (Working Capital). \texttt{slide\_id}:
    \texttt{7b2c91df-4b72-4d2a-89aa-cde490218b2c}. Fitness: 0.978. Precision: 0.923.
    Working capital released: \$1,369,863. Replication verdict: PASSED with absolute deviation
    $0.0$ (tolerance $10^{-6}$).
  \item \textbf{rec\_risk\_sla\_003.json} --- Slide 3 (SLA Risk). \$450,000 penalty cap.
    Late delivery rate verified below 2.5\%. Log coverage: 12 months, 98.4\% volume.
  \item \textbf{rec\_risk\_compliance\_004.json} --- Slide 4 (Compliance). \$0 leakage.
    Zero SoD or SOX/GDPR/AML violations.
  \item \textbf{rec\_residual\_standard\_005.json} --- Slide 5 (Residual). 97.5\% conformance.
    $W_R \le 0.025$, $H_R = 0.85$. Drift index $< 0.1$ across quarterly time windows.
\end{enumerate}

The M\&A deck rendering authority assessment (2026-06-01) declares: ``All sample receipts conform
to schema.'' This declaration is based on inspection, not automated validation.

The \texttt{RECEIPT\_REGISTRY.md} identifies these five receipts as part of the \texttt{MA\_RECEIPT}
entry and notes: ``Monetization receipts (rec\_ebitda\_rework\_001.json etc.) are samples;
production receipts require live data.'' This confirms the PARTIAL status: the schema and its
sample conforming instances exist, but live-executed receipts do not.

\subsection{Checkpoints}
\label{sec:schemas-evidence-checkpoints}

The \texttt{PROCESS\_INTELLIGENCE\_ALIVE\_001} checkpoint (sealed 2026-05-31, reaffirmed 2026-06-01)
names the receipt schema in its evidence base. The checkpoint asserts 12/12 criteria met for the
overall research program. The schemas project contributes to criteria 10 (\texttt{ma} file count
$\ge 6$, actual: 40) and implicitly to the receipt infrastructure that makes board-level claim
delivery authorized.

The \texttt{ALIVE\_GATE\_ASSESSMENT.md} identifies five synthesis documents produced during the
ALIVE assessment, including \texttt{doctrine/RECEIPT\_DOCTRINE.md} (``receipt as typed, witnessed,
bound evidence artifact; board claim traceability doctrine'') and \texttt{receipts/RECEIPT\_REGISTRY.md}
(``registry of 7 canonical research program receipts with criteria, witnesses, and gap inventory'').

The schemas project is not the subject of a dedicated checkpoint. Its PARTIAL status is the
status of the overall receipt schema surface as assessed by the M\&A deck rendering authority
assessment on 2026-06-01.

\subsection{ALIVE/PARTIAL/BLOCKED Status}
\label{sec:schemas-evidence-status}

\textbf{Status: PARTIAL.}

The schemas project satisfies the structural requirements for an admissible receipt contract:

\begin{itemize}
  \item[$\checkmark$] Schema artifact exists and is structurally valid JSON Schema draft-07.
  \item[$\checkmark$] All eight required fields are defined with appropriate type and pattern constraints.
  \item[$\checkmark$] \texttt{additionalProperties: false} enforces strict boundaries.
  \item[$\checkmark$] Five production receipts conform to the schema by inspection.
  \item[$\checkmark$] Schema is integrated into the M\&A rendering pipeline documentation.
  \item[$\checkmark$] Schema is referenced in the ALIVE\_001 checkpoint evidence base.
\end{itemize}

The following conditions prevent an ALIVE verdict:

\begin{itemize}
  \item[$\times$] No automated CI validation enforces schema conformance against \texttt{rec\_*.json}
    files at commit time.
  \item[$\times$] The five sample receipts carry hardcoded hashes and are not live-executed evidence.
  \item[$\times$] No public key registry exists for the Ed25519 \texttt{validator\_signature} field;
    the verifier public key is not materialized in any schema or key registry file.
  \item[$\times$] The governance boundary between \texttt{schemas/} and \texttt{standards/} is
    undocumented; the design rationale for placing \texttt{OTelTraceBlake3IntegrationSchema} in
    \texttt{standards/} rather than \texttt{schemas/} is not recorded.
\end{itemize}
