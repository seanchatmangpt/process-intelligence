\section{Contribution to the Dissertation}
\label{sec:comparisons-contribution}

\subsection{Contribution to Prediction vs.~Coordination}
\label{sec:comparisons-contribution-prediction}

The central tension in the dissertation is between \emph{prediction} (a system
that outputs a probability estimate about future behavior) and
\emph{coordination} (a system that enforces lawful behavior at the structural
level, refusing to proceed when invariants are violated). The comparisons corpus
provides the sharpest empirical demonstration of this tension in the process
mining domain.

PM4Py is a prediction-adjacent tool: it computes conformance scores, discovers
process models, and produces quality metrics, but it does not coordinate anything
--- it accepts any input, returns any output, and leaves enforcement to the caller.
wasm4pm is a coordination-adjacent tool: its WASM linear memory layout, cycle
budgets, and BLAKE3/Ed25519 hash chaining enforce structural properties at
runtime. But it still accepts raw \texttt{EventLog} instances without admission,
placing coordination responsibility on the caller.

wasm4pm-compat is the dissertation's clearest example of compiler-level
coordination: the \texttt{Evidence<T, State, W>} type with its closed
\texttt{pub(crate)} \texttt{Admitted} constructor moves the coordination point
from runtime to compile time. The comparisons corpus provides the three-way
table that makes this distinction precise and source-traceable: prediction
systems return values; coordination systems refuse to compile.

\subsection{Contribution to Process Evidence}
\label{sec:comparisons-contribution-evidence}

The comparisons corpus defines what counts as lawful process evidence in the
dissertation's framework. \texttt{Evidence<T, Admitted, W>} is the only carrier
type that satisfies the evidence standard: it binds a payload \texttt{T}, an
admission state \texttt{Admitted} (enforced by the compiler), and a witness
\texttt{W} (a named paper or standard). This is not a runtime certificate; it
is a type-level proof.

The corpus establishes that PM4Py evidence is not lawful process evidence in this
sense: a \texttt{dict} returned by \texttt{conformance\_diagnostics\_token\_based\_replay}
carries no admission state and no named witness. wasm4pm evidence is not lawful
process evidence either, because raw \texttt{EventLog} instances may enter
algorithm functions without passing through an admission gate. Only wasm4pm-compat
evidence satisfies the standard.

The lattice compliance model further shows that process evidence can be
\emph{cryptographically sealed}: the BLAKE3 hash chain $H_k =
\text{BLAKE3}(H_{k-1} \| e_k \| W_k)$, together with an Ed25519 signature over
$(H_n, W_n)$, produces a \texttt{Receipt} that provides mathematical proof of
process execution integrity. Tamper-resistance is not a post-hoc audit; it is
a structural property of the evidence chain.

\subsection{Contribution to Manufacturing Architecture}
\label{sec:comparisons-contribution-manufacturing}

The comparisons corpus contributes to the manufacturing architecture layer of the
dissertation by establishing the type-law boundary between the wasm4pm execution
engine and the wasm4pm-compat type-law enforcement layer. This boundary is the
admission gate: the point at which a raw process artifact becomes admitted process
evidence. The manufacturing pipeline does not begin until this gate is crossed.

The algorithm coverage matrix establishes the current coverage surface:
Alpha Miner and Heuristics Miner are available as full execution algorithms in
wasm4pm, with wasm4pm-compat carrying shape types for their outputs
(\texttt{WfNetConst<SOUNDNESS>} and \texttt{Dfg} respectively). Inductive Miner,
alignment conformance, POWL discovery, Declare checking, and OC-DFG are absent
from the wasm4pm execution engine; wasm4pm-compat carries shape types for all of
them, defining the reception surface for future execution algorithms.

The OCPQ query language (\texttt{ObjectScope}, \texttt{Predicate} tree,
\texttt{OcpqQuery}) is the manufacturing architecture's most forward-looking
contribution: it is a wasm4pm-compat-original query language with no execution
engine anywhere in the stack. Its presence in the type system defines a
manufacturing surface for future work.

\subsection{Contribution to Capital-Flow Infrastructure}
\label{sec:comparisons-contribution-capital}

The comparisons corpus contributes to the capital-flow and settlement layer of the
dissertation through the admission gate threshold structure materialized in the
conformance authority module. The Blue River Dam Gate 3 thresholds --- fitness
$\geq 0.95$ (automatic pass), $0.85 \leq$ fitness $< 0.95$ (conditional, board
override required), fitness $< 0.85$ (hard reject) --- are not business rules in
a configuration file; they are type-level constants compiled into the
\texttt{ConformanceAdmissionGate} struct.

A capital-flow settlement decision that depends on a conformance verdict can be
traced to a \texttt{ConformanceCertificate} carrying a Blake3 hash, an Ed25519
signature, and an \texttt{AdmissionVerdict} enum value (Pass, Conditional, Reject,
Unknown, Unavailable). The \texttt{Refusal<R, W>} architecture ensures that when
a settlement is refused, the refusal carries a specific named structural law
type, not a generic error string --- making the capital-flow refusal reason
machine-readable and auditable. This is the connection from the comparisons corpus
to the DAO and settlement layers of the thesis lineage (AUTHOR INTERPRETATION ---
no document in the comparisons directory itself names capital-flow infrastructure).
