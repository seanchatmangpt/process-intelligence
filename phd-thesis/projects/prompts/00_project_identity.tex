\section{Project Identity}
\label{sec:prompts-identity}

\subsection{Purpose}
\label{sec:prompts-identity-purpose}

The \texttt{prompts/} directory is the downstream manufacturing directive layer of the
process-intelligence research program. Its function is to translate verified research
findings from \texttt{doctrine/}, \texttt{gaps/}, \texttt{adversarial/}, and
\texttt{sources/} into actionable engineering specifications for three execution
targets: \texttt{wasm4pm} (the process-evidence execution engine),
\texttt{wasm4pm-compat} (the type-law admission crate), and \texttt{ggen} (the
M\&A claim manufacturing pipeline). No directive in this corpus authorizes independent
action; each carries an explicit authority source that traces to a research checkpoint
or gap register entry.

The corpus is sequenced and gated. The checkpoint file
\texttt{checkpoint\_\_downstream\_prompts\_complete.md} establishes a verification
checklist confirming that all twelve downstream directives are complete and free of
placeholders. Each directive carries a \texttt{Verdict} field---either
\emph{GRADUATION-READY / COMPLETE} or \emph{READY FOR ENGINEERING}---dated
2026-05-31. The master authorization gate for all downstream execution is the seal of
\texttt{PROCESS\_INTELLIGENCE\_ALIVE\_001}; the refactor mandate
(\texttt{downstream\_wasm4pm\_refactor.md}) makes this precondition explicit and
prohibits invocation before the seal exists.

\subsection{Repository Surface}
\label{sec:prompts-identity-surface}

The corpus contains 16 top-level prompt documents and 10 execution-plan blueprints
organized under \texttt{execution-plans/}. The top-level documents cover: wasm4pm
refactor, wasm4pm-compat gap closure, ggen projection integration, Blue River Dam
lifecycle authority, audit mesh expansion, M\&A deck manufacture, paper fixture
manufacture, PM4Py benchmark comparison, public standards expansion, and the
v30-execution mandate. The execution-plan blueprints provide mathematical and
cryptographic detail for: the Blue River Dam bridge, witness lattice, raw-laundering
block, ZKP execution boundaries, OCEL structural boundaries, lifecycle state
authority, mining authority boundary, loss policy, absence-proof fixtures, and WF-net
forgeability patch.

\subsection{Primary Research Role}
\label{sec:prompts-identity-role}

This corpus occupies the \emph{downstream-manufacturing-prompt-corpus} role in the
dissertation. It is not a standalone research contribution; it is the authorized
translation surface that makes research findings actionable. Each directive is the
formal output of a research phase: gap analysis authorizes gap-closure mandates;
conformance-authority analysis authorizes the refactor sequence; M\&A taxonomy
research authorizes the ggen integration specification. The corpus demonstrates that
process intelligence research can produce engineering-grade mandates with formal
authorization chains, gap-register traceability, and cryptographic proof requirements.

    an explicit completion criterion and fixture requirement.

  \item \texttt{DOWNSTREAM\_COMPAT\_GAP\_CLOSE.md} --- Four self-contained gap tasks

  \item \texttt{checkpoint\_\_downstream\_prompts\_complete.md} --- Master
    verification checklist confirming directive completeness across all twelve
    downstream targets.
\end{itemize}
