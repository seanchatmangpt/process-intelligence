\section{Open Questions and Next Work}
\label{sec:prompts-open-questions}

\subsection{Known Gaps}
\label{sec:prompts-open-questions-gaps}

Five structural gaps were identified during examination of the corpus.

\textbf{Gap 1: PROCESS\_INTELLIGENCE\_ALIVE\_001 seal not confirmed.}
The seal is the authorization gate for all downstream execution. It is cited as a
precondition in \texttt{downstream\_wasm4pm\_refactor.md} but no evidence of its
issuance appears within the \texttt{prompts/} directory. The seal must be confirmed
by examining \texttt{receipts/} and \texttt{checkpoints/} in the parent research
program before any downstream engineering directive is invoked. This is the highest
priority open question for the corpus's authorization status.

\textbf{Gap 2: AGI Court authority relationship is underdocumented.}
The \texttt{v30-execution-mandate.md} references an ``AGI Court'' that has determined
execution paths must adhere to Blue River Dam law. The mandate is seven lines and
lacks the mathematical depth of other directives. The authority relationship between
the AGI Court mandate and the gap register agent mandates is not specified. It is
unclear whether the AGI Court mandate supersedes, supplements, or merely reframes the
gap register directives. This is \textbf{AUTHOR THESIS / INTERPRETATION}: the AGI
Court construct appears to be an authority-naming convention for the research program's
own mandate-issuance process, not an external body.

\textbf{Gap 3: EBITDA figures are self-derived, not empirically grounded.}
The ggen integration directive maps fitness thresholds to EBITDA impact percentages
(fitness $\geq 0.95$ $\to$ $+5\%$, $f < 0.85$ $\to$ $-8\%$ to $-15\%$). These
figures are derived from the M\&A board claim taxonomy documents produced by the same
research program. No external empirical study is cited as the source of these
thresholds. This is an open question for the dissertation's capital-flow claims
chapter: the mapping from conformance metrics to financial impact requires independent
validation before board-admissible use.

\textbf{Gap 4: No ZKP gap document exists.}
\texttt{execution-plans/zkp-execution-boundaries.md} references SNARK/STARK proofs
attached to the execution context. Neither \texttt{wasm4pm} nor \texttt{wasm4pm-compat}
has ZKP infrastructure implemented, and no gap document appears to track this as a
formal gap. This is a \textbf{FUTURE WORK} item that requires a gap register entry
before it can be authorized for engineering.

\textbf{Gap 5: No benchmark receipts exist.}
The PM4Py benchmark comparison directive specifies success criteria (20--30$\times$
speedup) but these are targets, not achieved results. No benchmark execution receipts
exist in the \texttt{prompts/} directory or the referenced experiments directory.

\subsection{Research Risks}
\label{sec:prompts-open-questions-risks}

\textbf{Risk 1: Specification completeness without execution validation.}
The corpus claims ``99\% specification completeness'' (DOCTORAL THESIS confidence)
but no execution has occurred. Specification completeness and implementation
correctness are distinct properties. A directive can be formally complete while the
implementation it authorizes fails to satisfy the completion criterion. The risk is
that the corpus's confidence rating will be taken as evidence of implementation
readiness.

\textbf{Risk 2: Fitness threshold calibration.}
The fitness thresholds ($\geq 0.95$, $0.85$--$0.95$, $< 0.85$) are used consistently
across the lifecycle gate, ggen claim taxonomy, and M\&A risk framework. If these
thresholds are miscalibrated for a specific industry or process type, all downstream
claims derived from them will be systematically biased. No sensitivity analysis of
the thresholds is present in the corpus.

\textbf{Risk 3: ZKP theoretical exposure.}
The ZKP execution boundaries blueprint introduces SNARK/STARK proofs without an
implementation path. If this remains theoretical indefinitely, it constitutes an
unfalsifiable claim in the architecture description that could undermine the corpus's
credibility as an engineering specification.
