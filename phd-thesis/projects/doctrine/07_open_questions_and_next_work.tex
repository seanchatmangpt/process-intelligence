\subsection{Known Gaps}
\label{sec:doctrine-open-gaps}

The doctrine corpus is explicit and self-documenting about its structural gaps. This section
enumerates the five primary gaps in evidential priority order, following the hostile-witness
posture of the research program: gaps are reported to close them, not to minimize them.

\paragraph{GAP\_001 --- Compat-to-Wasm Bridge Missing (CRITICAL).}
This is the highest-priority structural gap blocking Level 5 process authority. The
\texttt{GraduateToWasm4pm} trait exists in wasm4pm-compat's \texttt{graduation} module but
wasm4pm has no corresponding import, no implementation, and no \texttt{wasm4pm-compat} entry
in its \texttt{Cargo.toml}. Wasm4pm algorithm functions accept raw \texttt{\&EventLog}
without passing through \texttt{Admit::admit()}: \texttt{discover\_ilp\_petri\_net\_from\_log},
\texttt{discover\_performance\_dfg\_from\_log}, and \texttt{discover\_simple\_process\_tree\_from\_log}
all take unadmitted input. Wasm4pm's refusal surface uses \texttt{ValidationError(String)}
catch-alls, the forbidden anti-pattern. The consequence is a two-universe problem:
\texttt{Evidence\textless{}T, Admitted, Ocel20\textgreater{}} $\neq$ \texttt{EventLog} and
there is no translation layer in either codebase.

\paragraph{GAP\_002 --- Execution Layer Uniformly Missing.}
All fifteen algorithm families in the taxonomy show wasm4pm execution as \emph{Missing}.
The structural types exist in compat (for Alpha Miner, Inductive Miner, Token Replay,
Alignment-Based Conformance, POWL, DFG, DECLARE, Log Skeleton, OCPQ, OC-Petri Mining) but
no execution authority has been implemented. The entire discovery and conformance execution
layer is structurally specified but not operational. This gap is a consequence of GAP\_001:
until the bridge exists, execution cannot receive admitted evidence.

\paragraph{GAP\_003 --- Three Witness Types Unimplemented.}
\texttt{DivergenceWitness} and \texttt{ConvergenceWitness} are needed to ground the OCEL 2.0
supremacy claim in \texttt{OBJECT\_CENTRIC\_SUPREMACY.md}. \texttt{OcDfgEdge\textless{}ObjectType\textgreater{}}
with \texttt{PhantomData} marker is needed for OC-DFG grounding. These types are declared in
doctrine but have no corresponding implementation receipts in the wasm4pm-compat source.

\paragraph{GAP\_004 --- WASM Boundary Law and BPMN OR-Join Doctrine Without Test Fixtures.}
The WASM boundary law (\texttt{wasm-boundary-law.md}, specifying the Specta and tsify
serialization constraints) and the BPMN OR-Join Smart-Completion doctrine
(\texttt{bpmn-or-join-completion.md}) exist as specifications but have no corresponding
compile-fail tests, compile-pass fixtures, or receipt chain. They are doctrine without
execution evidence, and therefore PARTIAL by the ALIVE gate definition.

\paragraph{GAP\_005 --- Upstream and Downstream Lineage Ends Not Documented in Doctrine.}
The thesis lineage maps the 2016 LM experiment $\to$ Chatman Equation $\to$ receipts $\to$
ggen $\to$ wasm4pm $\to$ Post-Cyberpunk PCP $\to$ AI XYNZ $\to$ capital-flow. The upstream
end (the 2016 LM experiment evidence) and the downstream ends (PCP, XYNZ, capital-flow
surfaces) are referenced in the thesis lineage but are not documented within the doctrine
corpus itself. No doctrine file cites the specific 2016 experiment artifacts or links to the
PCP/XYNZ repositories with receipt-bearing evidence.

\subsection{Deferred Gates}
\label{sec:doctrine-open-deferred}

Three gate conditions are deferred pending GAP\_001 resolution:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Algorithm Execution Gates}: No algorithm family may be declared ALIVE until
    wasm4pm implements the bridge intake for that family's \texttt{GraduationCandidate}.
    All fifteen families are currently deferred.
  \item \textbf{Decommission and Archive Receipts}: The two terminal lifecycle stages have
    no receipt-bearing execution evidence. A process instance cannot be declared fully closed
    until closure receipts exist for these stages.
  \item \textbf{M\&A Deck Manufacturing Receipt}: The five board-admissibility criteria
    for the M\&A deck manufacturing algorithm are specified in doctrine, but no deck has been
    manufactured with a receipted output tracing to admitted process evidence. This is the
    highest-value projection surface and the highest-priority deferred manufacturing gate.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Research Risks}
\label{sec:doctrine-open-risks}

Three research risks are identified from the doctrine corpus evidence:

\paragraph{Risk 1 --- Two-Universe Divergence.} The longer wasm4pm and wasm4pm-compat evolve
independently, the larger the translation cost of closing GAP\_001. If wasm4pm adds
algorithm functions that take raw \texttt{EventLog} parameters, and wasm4pm-compat adds new
witness markers, the bridge will require increasingly complex type-erasure logic. Every
release cycle without GAP\_001 closure increases the structural debt.

\paragraph{Risk 2 --- Doctrine Inflation Without Execution.} The doctrine corpus is growing:
34 files and 25 examined for this chapter. If doctrine continues to be manufactured without
corresponding execution receipts, the gap between the formal specification and the operational
surface will widen. Doctrine that cannot be actuated is documentation. The autonomic
actuation law is clear: knowledge that cannot actuate with receipt and replay is PARTIAL.

\paragraph{Risk 3 --- Partial Heuristics Miner Structural Coverage.} The Heuristics Miner
(Weijters and Ribeiro 2011) requires a \texttt{CausalNet} structural type that is not yet
typed in compat. Unlike other algorithm families where compat structural coverage is complete,
this family has a structural gap \emph{before} the execution gap. If the algorithm taxonomy
is cited in M\&A diligence without this distinction, the claim would be overstated.
