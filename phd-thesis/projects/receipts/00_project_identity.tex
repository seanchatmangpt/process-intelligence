\section{Project Identity}
\label{sec:receipts-identity}

\subsection{Purpose}
\label{sec:receipts-identity-purpose}

The receipts corpus is the cryptographic anchor layer for the entire process-intelligence research
program. Its purpose is to make every board-admissible claim in the doctoral research program
independently verifiable and tamper-evident. Rather than asserting that a process-mining result
is correct, the receipts corpus demands that each result be bound to a specific event log hash,
a specific process model hash, a specific fitness and precision score, and a validator signature
before any claim may enter the doctoral or commercial record. Without this anchoring layer, results
produced by \texttt{wasm4pm} and analyzed through \texttt{pm4py} would remain asserted values with
no durable chain of custody. The receipts corpus converts those assertions into receipted facts.

The corpus also serves a secondary purpose as the integrity boundary between research and
execution. Wasm4pm modules are certified by generation receipts before their outputs may feed
into downstream M\&A claims. The M\&A claim receipts themselves carry fitness scores, precision
scores, and replication verdicts. The entire system is designed so that a claim cannot flow from
manufacturing to board presentation unless a receipt exists that anchors it to evidence.

\subsection{Repository Surface}
\label{sec:receipts-identity-surface}

The receipts directory at \texttt{/Users/sac/process-intelligence/receipts/} contains fifteen

The second layer is the four wasm4pm module generation receipts:
\texttt{wasm4pm\_conformance\_authority\_generation.md},
\texttt{wasm4pm\_mining\_generation.md}, \texttt{wasm4pm\_replay\_generation.md}, and
\texttt{wasm4pm\_lifecycle\_generation.md}. These certify that the Rust module manufacturing
pipeline compiled, tested, and sealed each module before its outputs were used.

The third layer is the governance and ledger documents:
\texttt{RECEIPT\_REGISTRY.md}, \texttt{ma\_deck\_rendering\_authority\_assessment.md}, and
the Blue River Dam orchestrator generation receipt \texttt{blue\_river\_generation.md}.

\subsection{Primary Research Role}
\label{sec:receipts-identity-role}

Within the dissertation, the receipts corpus plays the role of \emph{cryptographic-receipt-chain-and-claim-provenance}. It is the evidence layer that makes the thesis argument falsifiable. The doctoral claim is that process intelligence, as implemented through \texttt{wasm4pm} and anchored by this receipt chain, produces board-admissible results that meet or exceed the fitness threshold of $0.95$ and precision threshold of $0.90$ required for M\&A due diligence. The receipts corpus is the only artifact in the research program that can confirm or refute this claim through inspectable, signed, hash-bound documents.

The corpus also establishes the boundary between the research program and downstream execution. No wasm4pm module may be represented as production-ready unless its generation receipt exists and is sealed. No M\&A financial claim may be represented as board-admissible unless its JSON receipt exists with a passing \texttt{board\_admissibility} verdict. This boundary is enforced by the \texttt{ConformanceAdmissionGate} (Blue River Dam Gate 3) in the conformance authority module.
