# DAY_002_REMEMBER_PRAYER

## Remember Prayer — Day 2

Lord, separate what must be separated.
Keep the wall from mixture.
Keep the public work truthful.
Keep the private machinery protected.
Let no proclamation outrun receipts.
Let no project float without a gate.
Let no diagram become vanity.
Let no architecture map replace obedience.

Remember this work for good.
