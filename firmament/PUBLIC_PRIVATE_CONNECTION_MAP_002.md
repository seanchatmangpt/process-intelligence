## Connection Rules

Seven connection rules governing the public/private boundary:

1. **LinkedIn → Public Wall Only**: Posts may teach doctrine but must not link to private repo internals. Only content from ALIVE-gated projects with sealed receipts may appear in LinkedIn content. UNKNOWN and ABSENT projects contribute nothing to the LinkedIn surface until an ALIVE verdict is issued.

2. **Newsletter → Water Gate Teaching**: Water Gate content is public doctrine, not implementation. Newsletter issues teach the principles of process intelligence (conformance, lifecycle soundness, object-centric event logs) without exposing the specific algorithms, receipt chains, or gap remediation strategies that implement those principles.

3. **Manifesto → Sheep Gate Consecration**: The manifesto declares what is at stake — the category claim for full-lifecycle process intelligence — not how the engine works. The manifesto may reference that proof gates exist and that the wall is being built; it must not describe gate implementation, internal receipt formats, or competitive algorithm details.

4. **Landing Page → Fish Gate Entry**: The landing page is the entry point to the public wall. It shows that the wall exists, what it stands for, and who it is for. It must not link to private repositories, expose internal project states, or reveal the manufacturing pipeline behind the public canon.

5. **Public Canon → Doctrine Without Machinery**: Theorem sets, doctrine files, and category definitions teach the law of process intelligence. The algorithm code that implements those theorems stays private. Publishing the theorem set is not the same as publishing the implementation — only the former is permitted on the public canon surface.

6. **C4 Diagrams → Architecture Without Secrets**: C4 diagrams may show containers, flows, and system boundaries at the appropriate level of abstraction. No code-level secrets, active competitive advantage algorithms, internal receipt formats, or unpublished architectural control points may appear in any C4 diagram on a public surface. C4 diagrams for UNKNOWN-state projects must not be published.

7. **Receipt Ledger → Proof Without Exposure**: Receipts prove ALIVE status and are publishable as proof signals. They must not expose private keys, internal manufacturing templates, unpublished work, gap details, or project-internal control structures. A receipt summary (project name, gate reached, date, verdict) is public; a full receipt with internal chain details is private.
