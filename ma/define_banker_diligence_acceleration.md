# Banker Diligence Acceleration

Traditional M&A due diligence relies on manual interviews, sample-based audits, and spreadsheets, resulting in long deal cycles and high transaction risk. By utilizing automated process intelligence, investment bankers and transaction advisors can significantly accelerate diligence timelines, improve Quality of Earnings (QofE) validation, and automate the creation of audit-ready decks.

## 1. Due Diligence Timeline Comparison

Process intelligence shifts due diligence from subjective interviews to automated empirical analysis:

```
Traditional Diligence:
[Weeks 1-3: Interviews & Questionnaires] -> [Weeks 4-6: Sample Audits] -> [Week 7+: Manual Slide Writing]

Accelerated Diligence:
[Day 1: Ingest XES/OCEL Logs] -> [Day 2: Run Conformance & Latency Queries] -> [Day 3: Auto-Generate Verified Slides]
```

## 2. Key Acceleration Mechanisms

### A. Automated EBITDA & QofE Adjustments
* **Mechanism**: Bankers use process mining to identify structural inefficiencies (e.g., manual corrections, double-billing) and automatically calculate EBITDA adjustments.
* **Benefit**: QofE teams can immediately prove "run-rate adjustments" using event log counts of manual interventions rather than making qualitative estimates.

### B. The Self-Documenting Slide Deck
* **Mechanism**: M&A presentation slide build systems pull data directly from the verified execution receipts generated by the `wasm4pm` engine.
* **Benefit**: The diligence deck's appendix is automatically populated with cryptographic hashes, query definitions, and conformance proofs, eliminating the need to manually verify slide claims.

### C. Red-Flag Filtering (Continuous Conformance)
* **Mechanism**: Instead of auditing a sample of 50 invoices, the execution core runs Linear Temporal Logic (LTL) check scripts over the entire event log of 500,000 invoices in seconds.
* **Benefit**: compliance and risk red flags (e.g., sanctions violations, unauthorized price overrides) are detected on day one, avoiding late-stage deal breakage.
