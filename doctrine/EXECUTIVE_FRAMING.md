# Process Intelligence — Executive Framing

## The Problem

The enterprise does not have a data problem.
The enterprise has a consequence-conservation problem.

Every AI system in the enterprise produces outputs.
None of them can prove that those outputs became lawful consequence.

## The Gap

AI tells you what might happen.
Process Intelligence tells you what actually happened — and proves it.

## The Claim

Process Intelligence is the missing discipline between AI output and receipted consequence.

## The Threat Without It

- Audit failures that cannot be explained by event logs
- AI-assisted decisions that cannot be replayed or verified
- Process conformance that degrades silently after deployment
- Consequence claims that collapse under adversarial review

## The Offer

A receipted process intelligence layer that:
1. Admits evidence lawfully (Admission Gate)
2. Derives process truth from event logs (wasm4pm)
3. Bounds execution change (CONSTRUCT8)
4. Issues verifiable receipts (BLAKE3)
5. Supports full replay audit
