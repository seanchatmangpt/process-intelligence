# Full-Lifecycle Process Intelligence (FLPI)

## 1. The Imperative of Total Observation
A process unobserved is a process compromised. FLPI asserts that intelligence must span the entire timeline of a process—from its dark-state genesis to its cryptographic terminus. 

## 2. The FLPI Continuum
1.  **Dark State (Genesis)**: The theoretical origin of intent. We cannot measure it, but we prepare the landing zone.
2.  **Ontological Emergence**: The moment a process requests state formulation. Checked against the `ostar-governor`.
3.  **Actuation (The Forge)**: The process manipulates reality/data. Monitored strictly by `ostar-auditor` mechanisms.
4.  **Audit & Receipt**: Immutable OTel traces and BLAKE3 receipts are generated. The process is now *historical fact*.
5.  **Terminus (Closure)**: Verification that the process completed its intended state transition without dropping logic (`ostar-doctor`).

## 3. AGI-Adversarial Posture
At every stage, FLPI assumes a hostile AGI is attempting to hijack the state transition. Total observation prevents shadow-states and ghost-processes.
