# Reverse Lock-In (The Ontological Trap)

## 1. The Philosophy of the Trap
Traditional software attempts to lock the user in with proprietary formats and walled gardens. This is brittle. **Reverse Lock-In** is the strategy of locking the *adversary* out by open-sourcing the ultimate, unforgeable standard of truth.

## 2. Mechanism of Action
By establishing a hyper-narrow, mathematically provable corridor of operational truth (via public standards), any deviation by an adversarial AGI or competitor becomes instantly visible and fatal to their system's integrity.

## 3. The Paradigm
We do not build a cage for the customer. We build the laws of physics. The competitor is forced to use our physics to exist. If they attempt to rewrite the physics, their internal logic collapses. We have reversed the lock-in from the user to the underlying operational reality.
