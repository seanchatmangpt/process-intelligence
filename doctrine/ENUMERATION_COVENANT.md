# Enumeration Covenant

## The Covenant

No project may claim wall membership without being named in the ledger before construction begins.

Every builder, every wall section, every gate — named and enumerated before the first stone is laid. The discipline of naming is not administrative overhead; it is the condition of the wall's integrity. Undocumented segments become attack surfaces.

## Canonical Statement

> Nehemiah 52 is the wall-building discipline layer — the enumeration covenant that names every project, every gate, every builder, and every section before construction begins. It is the reason the ledger exists at all: no project may claim wall membership without being named here.

## Operating Principles

### Principle 1: Naming Before Building

A project that cannot be named in the ledger before it is built has no wall standing. The ledger is not a retrospective report; it is a prerequisite for construction.

### Principle 2: No Anonymous Builders

Every contribution to the wall must be attributed. Anonymity is an anti-pattern. A wall section without a named builder is a gap in the enumeration covenant, not a gap in the wall — the wall simply does not extend there.

### Principle 3: No Self-Referential ALIVE Claims

A project may not claim ALIVE status using the ledger that contains the claim. The ALIVE verdict requires an independent project container at a canonical path with its own receipt. A ledger entry is not a project; a project is not a ledger entry.

Corollary: FIRMAMENT_PROJECT_SPR_LEDGER_002.md formerly contained a self-referential ALIVE claim for nehemiah-52. That claim was voided and replaced with `ABSENT`. The resolution created `/Users/sac/nehemiah-52` as an independent project container.

### Principle 4: Gate Assignment Is Permanent

Once a project is assigned to a gate, that assignment is permanent for the lifetime of the 52-day enumeration. Gate reassignment requires a formal wall-doctrine commit with documented rationale.

### Principle 5: Receipt Before Public Claim

No public claim may be made on behalf of a project until the project's gate receipt is issued. The Fish Gate is the entry point for public claims. Nothing passes the Fish Gate without a receipt.
