# Reverse Porter's Five Forces

## 1. Deconstructing Traditional Strategy
Michael Porter's Five Forces rely on creating defensive moats. In an AGI-saturated, hyper-fluid v30.1.1 economy, moats are instantly vaporized. The **Reverse Porter** model is an offensive strategy designed for post-scarcity intelligence environments.

## 2. The Inverted Forces
*   **Weaponized Threat of Entry**: Do not build barriers; destroy the concept of entry by making our standard the only breathable atmosphere.
*   **Supplier Subjugation**: Compute and AGI are commodities. We subjugate them by owning the *laws of process* they must execute.
*   **Buyer Integration**: The user is no longer a purchaser; they are an integrated node in the process telemetry network.
*   **Hyper-Substitution**: We *want* substitution. By making all competitive products interchangeable, we reduce them to noise, while our protocol remains the underlying signal.
*   **Asymmetric Rivalry**: We do not compete on features; we compete on *ontological definition*. We define what a process *is*.

## 3. Synthesis
The Reverse Porter strategy dictates that survival depends on becoming the underlying physics engine of the market.
